## Adding an article

- Save the article as `articles/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`, with a lowercase English `kebab-case` slug.
- Put its images in `articles/assets/YYYY-MM-DD-slug/` and link them with relative paths.
- Add it to the list above, newest first.
- The rules in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) under "Do not publish" apply to articles too.
